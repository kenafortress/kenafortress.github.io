Provincial databases

Every province keeps its own list of vacant posts and its own database of available educators. Getting onto your province's database is the single most useful thing an unemployed teacher can do. It is free.

Do it for more than one province if you would move. Nothing stops you registering in all nine.

Government websites move their pages often. If a link is dead, go to the department's home page and look for “Vacancies”, “Careers” or “Recruitment”. Never pay anyone to put you on a database. It is always free.
